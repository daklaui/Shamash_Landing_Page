# Website Structure

## Primary Goal

The website must guide each visitor to the right Shamash IT solution quickly.

It should work as an enterprise gateway:

- Banks and financial institutions find XpertFactor.
- Business owners and finance teams find Factorix.
- Organizations digitizing documents find Document Management.
- Companies automating workflows find AI Automation and Digital Transformation.
- Organizations needing custom systems find Enterprise Web and Mobile.

## Homepage First Viewport

The first viewport must include:

- Clear Shamash IT positioning
- Hero slide focused on one solution at a time
- Visible slider controls
- At least one strong CTA
- A visible hint of the next section
- Bright enterprise styling

## Audience Router

Place the audience router immediately after the hero or partially visible beneath it.

Required audience cards:

- I am a Bank or Financial Institution -> XpertFactor
- I Manage a Business or Company -> Factorix
- I Need Document Management -> Document Management
- I Want to Automate Processes -> AI Automation
- I Need Custom Software or a Mobile App -> Enterprise Web and Mobile

Each card should include:

- Audience title
- Recommended solution
- Short business problem
- Three capability tags
- CTA
- Accessible focus state

## Solution Page Pattern

Each solution page should include:

1. Solution hero
2. Audience and problem statement
3. Product dashboard or workflow visual
4. Business outcomes
5. Feature groups
6. Workflow or architecture section
7. Security and reliability notes
8. Integration capabilities
9. Case study or proof section
10. FAQ
11. CTA

## Services Page Pattern

The services page must show Shamash IT's capability depth.

Recommended sections:

1. Service page hero
2. Four primary service pillars
3. Detailed service catalog
4. Delivery process
5. Technology stack
6. Managed support model
7. CTA

## Footer

Use Midnight Navy for the footer.

Footer should include:

- Logo

- Solutions links
- Services links
- Industries links
- Company links
