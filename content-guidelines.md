# Content Guidelines

## Core Message

Use this message consistently:

> Shamash IT transforms complex business operations into secure, connected, and intelligent digital systems.

## Voice

The copy should feel:

- Professional
- Clear
- Confident
- Human
- Enterprise-focused
- Practical
- Trustworthy

Avoid copy that feels:

- Buzzword-heavy
- Too technical for decision makers
- Generic agency language
- Artificially futuristic
- Overpromising
- Cheap or salesy

## Messaging Hierarchy

Lead with business outcomes, then explain the technology.

Preferred structure:

1. Audience
2. Problem
3. Business value
4. Solution
5. Capabilities
6. Proof or credibility
7. CTA

## Homepage Headline Options

Preferred:

> Enterprise Software and AI Automation for Modern Business Operations

Alternative:

> Secure, Connected, and Intelligent Digital Systems for Enterprises

Alternative:

> Technology Platforms That Help Businesses Operate With More Control

Do not use:

- We build websites
- We are the best IT company
- Transform your future with innovation
- Unlock the power of AI

## Writing Rules

- Keep headlines specific.
- Keep paragraphs short.
- Use concrete business language.
- Use capability lists for scanning.
- Use "secure", "connected", and "intelligent" carefully as recurring strategic terms.
- Explain technical terms through business value.
- Avoid exaggerated claims unless proof is provided.
