# Shamash IT Brand Guidelines

## Company

Company name: Shamash IT

Industry: Information Technology, Enterprise Software, FinTech, Digital Transformation, AI Automation, Cloud Infrastructure, Managed IT Services

Primary markets:

- Tunisia
- Africa
- Europe

## Mission

Shamash IT helps organizations transform complex, manual, and fragmented operations into secure, connected, and intelligent digital systems.

The company designs and implements enterprise software, financial technology platforms, ERP solutions, AI-powered automation, cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, and managed IT services that improve efficiency, visibility, data security, and decision-making.

## Positioning

Shamash IT must be perceived as a reliable strategic technology partner for enterprise digital transformation.

It is not positioned as:

- A generic web agency
- A low-cost development shop
- A simple website provider
- A trendy AI experiment company

It is positioned as:

- A specialized enterprise software company
- A digital-transformation consultancy
- A FinTech software specialist
- A long-term managed technology partner

## Target Audiences

- Factoring companies and financial institutions
- Banks and enterprise finance departments
- Small, medium, and large companies seeking digital transformation
- Organizations automating repetitive processes with AI
- Companies requiring custom web applications, business platforms, or corporate websites
- Businesses managing invoices, quotations, purchase orders, payments, inventory, and cash flow
- Organizations needing document digitization, archiving, OCR, workflows, and secure data management
- Companies needing ERP implementation, integration, or optimization
- Organizations requiring IT outsourcing, infrastructure management, and technical support
- Businesses seeking server administration, cybersecurity, cloud infrastructure, monitoring, and backup
- Executives, finance directors, operations managers, and IT directors seeking efficiency, visibility, and control

## Color System

The official Shamash IT palette is the single visual source of truth.

### Primary Brand Color

Shamash Blue

- HEX: `#35A8DE`
- RGB: `53, 168, 222`

Usage:

- Primary buttons
- Links
- Highlights
- Hero lighting accents
- Icons
- Interactive elements
- Dashboard accents
- Subtle gradients

### Secondary Brand Color

Enterprise Indigo

- HEX: `#505CA2`
- RGB: `80, 92, 162`

Usage:

- Secondary buttons
- Navigation accents
- Dark gradients
- Enterprise panels
- Technology diagrams
- Premium interface details

### Accent Color

Compass Gold

- HEX: `#E9AE11`
- RGB: `233, 174, 17`

Usage:

- Premium accents
- Important highlights
- Small decorative details
- Selected metrics
- Signature focus points

Compass Gold must stay rare. It should represent precision, guidance, leadership, and excellence.

### Dark Color

Midnight Navy

- HEX: `#242850`
- RGB: `36, 40, 80`

Usage:

- Headings
- Footer
- Conversion bands
- Selected premium panels
- High-contrast interface details

### Neutrals

- Pure White: `#FFFFFF`
- Light Background: `#F8FAFC`
- Background Gray: `#F3F4F6`
- Heading: `#111827`
- Body Text: `#374151`
- Secondary Text: `#6B7280`
- Border: `#D1D5DB`
- Light Border: `#E5E7EB`

## Color Ratio

Use:

- 70% white and neutral surfaces
- 20% Shamash Blue
- 8% Enterprise Indigo
- 2% Compass Gold

This ratio keeps the site bright, premium, and controlled.

## Gradients

Primary gradient:

```css
linear-gradient(135deg, #35A8DE 0%, #505CA2 100%)
```

Use for:

- Buttons
- Hero interface lighting
- Icon backgrounds
- Data visualizations
- Subtle dashboard highlights

Do not flood full sections with the gradient. Use it as a refined brand signal.

## Visual Style

Use:

- Bright neutral backgrounds
- Premium dashboard mockups
- Product interface previews
- Meaningful workflow diagrams
- Clean SVG icons
- Subtle blue-indigo gradients
- Minimal gold accents
- Soft shadows
- Precise spacing
- Modern typography

Avoid:

- Generic buildings
- Generic robots
- Cyberpunk scenes
- Dark futuristic backgrounds
- Unrelated AI imagery
- Stock-like decorative photos
- Product palettes that replace the core Shamash colors

## Reference Quality

Use these brands as quality references for restraint, clarity, and polish:

- Apple
- Stripe
- Microsoft
- IBM
- NVIDIA
- Adobe
- Linear
- Notion

Do not copy their designs. Use them only as quality benchmarks.
